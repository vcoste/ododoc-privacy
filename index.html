OdoDoc

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

OdoDoc is an iPhone app that scans documents, reads them on your device, and saves PDFs to your Google Drive. Vincent Coste makes OdoDoc. Contact: vincentcoste.4@gmail.com.

What the app handles

  • Your Google account email, so the app can sign you in and show which account is connected.
  • The pages you scan, the PDF it builds, and the details you confirm (title, date, document type, amounts, reminders).
  • A local copy of those documents on your iPhone, and the Google Drive folder OdoDoc creates (Personal Archive).

Where it goes

Sign-in and file upload go directly to Google, using Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API. The Drive permission is drive.file, which covers files OdoDoc creates. OdoDoc does not run a server for your documents, and it does not sell or share them with advertisers.

Text recognition runs on your iPhone.

Reminders are local notifications on your iPhone.

Tracking

OdoDoc does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.

How long it stays

Documents stay in the app until you delete them, and in your Google Drive until you delete them there. Signing out of OdoDoc removes the sign-in tokens from this iPhone and keeps the Drive files in your account. Removing the app deletes the local copies.

To ask a question about your data, or for help deleting something you cannot remove yourself, email vincentcoste.4@gmail.com.
